We live far away too.

We're Adriana and Robinson, siblings, and like many families, we learned to hold the distance together with short calls, photos lost in a chat thread, and the quiet guilt of not being there for our parents' everyday moments.

One day we decided that "being far" didn't have to mean "being absent." In English, kin means family, your people. Kinly is, literally, us: two kin who built something so that no family has to choose between distance and closeness.

KinlyBox isn't a company that sells boxes. It's a family helping other families stay present, no matter how many miles lie between you.

Questions, answered plainly

What's the difference between the Classic and Premium box?

Both include three curated products, two snacks, and a personalized letter with your family photo. Premium adds one more personalized detail, like a mug, a mini photo album or an engraved keychain, in a burgundy gift box. Classic is $46.99 a month and Premium is $54.99 a month.

How does the personalized letter work?

After you order, you fill in a short form (about five minutes) with your message and a photo you love. We print them together on one page and place it inside the box.

When do boxes ship?

Boxes ship once a month, around the 15th. Order by the 8th to be in that month's box. Our first boxes ship December 17.

Is shipping included?

Yes. Shipping within the United States is included in the price, so there's nothing extra to pay at checkout. We don't ship internationally yet.

Can I send a box to a grandparent in another city?

Yes, that's exactly what KinlyBox is for. You enter your loved one's address, and the box goes straight to their door anywhere in the United States.

What is KinlyBox Memories?

A yearly memory book that compiles every letter and photo from your boxes. It's included free with Always Family (12 months) and sold separately for $39.99.

Who is behind KinlyBox?

We're Adriana and Robinson, siblings who live far from our own parents. Write to us anytime at hello@kinlybox.com; we answer within 24 hours.
